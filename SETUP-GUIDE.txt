90 DAYS APP: SETUP GUIDE (about 15 minutes, one time)
=====================================================

You need: a computer, and your Android phone.

PART 1: Put the files on GitHub (computer)
------------------------------------------
1. Unzip 90-days-app.zip on your computer. You get a folder called "90-days-app".
2. Go to https://github.com and click "Sign up". Create a free account.
3. Click the "+" at the top right, then "New repository".
   - Repository name: 90-days-app
   - Choose "Public" (so your phone can download the app without logging in;
     no personal data is in these files)
   - Tick "Add a README file"
   - Click "Create repository"
4. On the new page, click "Add file", then "Upload files".
5. Open the "90-days-app" folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING inside it
   (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on Mac), and drag it into the GitHub page.
   Wait until all files finish loading.
6. Scroll down and click the green "Commit changes" button.
   Check the file list: you should see folders www, resources, scripts, keys.

PART 2: Start the automatic build (computer)
--------------------------------------------
7. Click "Add file", then "Create new file".
8. In the name box, type exactly:   .github/workflows/build.yml
   (GitHub turns each "/" into a folder as you type. That's correct.)
9. Open build-workflow.yml from your unzipped folder in Notepad (or TextEdit),
   copy all of it, and paste it into the big box on GitHub.
10. Click "Commit changes", then "Commit changes" again.
11. Click the "Actions" tab at the top. You'll see "Build 90 Days app" running
    (yellow dot). Wait about 8 to 12 minutes until it shows a green tick.
    If it shows a red X, take a screenshot and send it to Claude.

PART 3: Install on your phone
-----------------------------
12. On your phone, open Chrome and go to:
    https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/90-days-app/releases
    (replace YOUR-USERNAME with your GitHub username)
13. Tap the latest release, then tap "90-days.apk" to download it.
14. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installs from Chrome:
    tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", go back, tap Install.
    If Play Protect warns you, tap "More details" then "Install anyway".
    (It warns because the app isn't from the Play Store. It's your own app.)
15. Open "90 Days". Pick your Day 1, then tap "Turn on reminders" and Allow.

PART 4: Make reminders reliable (phone, 1 minute)
-------------------------------------------------
16. Phone Settings > Apps > 90 Days > Battery > choose "Unrestricted".
17. Xiaomi / Redmi / Poco, Oppo, Vivo, Realme: also turn on "Autostart"
    for 90 Days (Settings > Apps > 90 Days, or the Security app).
18. In the app: Me tab > "Send a test in 5 seconds". Close the app and wait.
    You should get a notification with a chime.

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Open the app at least once a week: it schedules reminders 10 days ahead.
- Keep using your normal alarm clock to wake up.
- Your data stays only on this phone. Use Me tab > "Copy backup" now and then
  and paste it somewhere safe (for example WhatsApp "Message yourself").
- Updates: if Claude gives you a new www/index.html, upload it to the www folder
  on GitHub (Add file > Upload files, inside www). A new build starts by itself.
  Install the new APK over the old one. Your data is kept.
